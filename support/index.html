玩家支持

棋子为什么不能移动？

只有横向连续至少三颗同色棋子组成整组。单颗或两颗移动后，必须立即横向成组；困难模式下，主动移动的棋子不能越界。教学关须按当前提示的棋子组与方向操作。无法移动时会弹窗说明原因。

如何切换语言？

从菜单打开“设置”，选择“语言”。可选择“跟随系统”或手动选定语言，手动选择会保存到设备。

购买了皮肤包，如何恢复？

在游戏商店点击“恢复购买”，使用原来购买时的 Apple 账号核验全部皮肤包权益。皮肤包只解锁外观，不含游戏币、道具或关卡。

游戏币能通过恢复购买找回吗？

游戏币是消耗型商品，不能通过恢复购买重建已完成交易的本地余额。请保留设备数据；删除应用及其数据可能丢失本地余额。如订单已确认但没有到账，请联系支持，提供发生时间及错误提示，不要附密码或完整支付信息。

更换设备后，进度会自动同步吗？

目前没有游戏自己的跨设备同步功能。存档保存在本机，系统备份及恢复取决于设备设置。遇到问题时，请先联系支持，避免直接清空数据或删除应用。

如何反馈故障？

请通过下方邮箱说明游戏版本、iOS版本、设备型号、难度与关卡，以及复现步骤。可以附上不含敏感内容的截图。无需提供 Apple 密码或付款账号。

Player support

Why can’t a tile move?

Only horizontal runs of at least three matching tiles form a group. A single tile or pair must form a horizontal group immediately after moving. In Hard mode, active tiles cannot cross the edge. Tutorial levels require the instructed group and direction. Rejected moves show an explanation.

How do I change language?

Open Settings from the menu and choose Language. Use Follow System or select a language manually; your choice is saved on the device.

How do I restore the skin pack?

Tap Restore Purchases in the game’s shop using the Apple Account that made the purchase. The All Skins Pack unlocks appearances only, not coins, tools or levels.

Can Restore Purchases recover my coins?

Coins are consumable products. Restore Purchases cannot rebuild a local balance from completed coin transactions. Keep your device data: deleting the app and its data may remove the local balance. If an approved order has not been credited, contact support with the time and any error message, without passwords or full payment details.

Does progress automatically sync to another device?

The game currently has no game-managed cross-device sync. Saves are local; system backup and recovery depend on device settings. Contact support before clearing data or deleting the app when troubleshooting.

How do I report a problem?

Email the game version, iOS version, device model, mode and level, and steps to reproduce the problem. You may attach a screenshot without sensitive information. Do not send Apple passwords or payment-account details.
